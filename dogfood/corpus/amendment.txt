AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO THE MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") modifies the Master Services Agreement between the parties. Capitalized terms not
defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement. Where this Amendment conflicts with the Agreement, this Amendment
controls.

1. Section 2 (Term and Renewal) is amended so that the notice period required to prevent automatic renewal is changed
from sixty (60) days to ninety (90) days' prior written notice.

2. Section 5 (Governing Law) is amended in its entirety: this Agreement is now governed by the laws of the State of
Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

3. A new late-payment term is added: invoices not paid when due accrue a late charge of one and one-half percent (1.5%)
per month on the outstanding balance until paid.

4. Except as expressly amended above, all other terms and conditions of the Agreement remain in full force and effect.
