MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between the parties as of the Effective Date. The
specific services to be performed are described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW") executed under this Agreement;
in the event of a conflict between this Agreement and a SOW, this Agreement controls unless the SOW expressly states
otherwise.

Section 2. Term and Renewal. The initial term of this Agreement is two (2) years, commencing on January 1, 2026 (the
"Effective Date"). Thereafter, the Agreement renews automatically for successive one-year terms unless either party
provides sixty (60) days' prior written notice of non-renewal.

Section 3. Fees and Payment. Fees for the services are $12,000 per month, invoiced monthly and payable net thirty (30)
days from the invoice date. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes.

Section 5. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New
York, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

Section 7. Limitation of Liability. Except for breaches of confidentiality, neither party's aggregate liability under
this Agreement will exceed the total fees paid by the Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise
to the claim.

Section 9. Confidentiality. Each party will protect the other's Confidential Information using no less than reasonable
care. This Section survives termination.
