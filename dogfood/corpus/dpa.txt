DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") supplements the Master Services Agreement and applies whenever the Provider
processes Personal Data on behalf of the Client. In the event of a conflict between this DPA and the Agreement with
respect to the processing of Personal Data, this DPA controls.

Security Incident Notification. The Provider will notify the Client of a confirmed personal-data breach without undue
delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of it.

Sub-processors. The Provider will not engage any sub-processor to process Personal Data without the Client's prior
written consent. A current list of approved sub-processors is maintained on request.

Return and Deletion. Upon termination or expiry of the Agreement, the Provider will, at the Client's choice, return or
delete all Personal Data within thirty (30) days, except where retention is required by law.

International Transfers. The Provider will not transfer Personal Data outside the country of collection without an
approved transfer mechanism.
