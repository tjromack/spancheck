STATEMENT OF WORK NO. 1

This Statement of Work ("SOW") is executed under, and incorporates by reference, the Master Services Agreement between
the parties. Capitalized terms not defined in this SOW have the meaning given in the Agreement.

Scope. The Provider will design and deliver a data-integration platform consisting of three deliverables: (a) a landing
and staging pipeline; (b) a reconciliation and quality-gate layer; and (c) a read API with published documentation.

Delivery Milestones. The work is delivered in three milestones — Milestone 1 (staging pipeline), Milestone 2
(reconciliation layer), and Milestone 3 (read API). Acceptance of each milestone is subject to the Client's review
within ten (10) business days of delivery.

Milestone Payments. Fees for this SOW are payable against milestones as follows: forty percent (40%) on acceptance of
Milestone 1, thirty percent (30%) on acceptance of Milestone 2, and thirty percent (30%) on acceptance of Milestone 3.
These milestone payments are in addition to the recurring monthly fees under the Agreement.

Personnel. The Provider will assign a technical lead for the duration of this SOW.
